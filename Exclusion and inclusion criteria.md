### Inclusion criteria

- Studies addressing sustainability in software development or software-intensive organizations.
- Studies providing empirical evidence on sustainability implementation practices, organizational arrangements, or factors affecting implementation.
- Studies addressing environmental or other sustainability dimensions with a clear connection to software engineering.
- Studies using empirical methods, such as surveys, interviews, case studies, experiments, mixed methods, or repository and artifact analyses.

### Exclusion criteria

- Conceptual, theoretical, or vision papers without empirical evidence.
- Proposed frameworks or methods without empirical evaluation.
- Studies focusing only on software maintainability, longevity, or quality without an explicit sustainability perspective.
- Studies discussing sustainability at a general organizational level without a clear connection to software development or software-intensive organizations.
- Duplicate records.
